🔄 Vida Real Estate Lifecycle Stages (Detailed Business Process Flows)

Step-by-Step Operations

Sequential operational workflows across all 6 customer lifecycle stages. Each stage maps customer interactions, internal decisions, and business milestone transitions.

Stage 01 Pre-Sales & Lead Qualification Flow
Ready
1 Inquiry Intake

Fresh customer inquiries land into our central system from digital channels (Meta Ads, 99acres portal, or website forms).

Example Inquiry received: "Amit Sharma, +91-98765-43210, looking for a 3 BHK in Sector 62 under ₹1.5 Cr".
2 Identity & Profile Match

System checks phone & email against our database to de-duplicate contacts and merge past interaction history.

Example Match found: Amit visited Tower A in 2024. Prior notes merged so he isn't treated as a stranger.
3 Priority & Intent Scoring

Scoring engine calculates budget alignment against available inventory, loan readiness, and purchase timeline.

Example Score: 92/100 (High Priority) — Budget matches Tower B 3 BHK units; home loan is pre-approved.
4 AI Voice Call Outreach

We dispatch the qualified lead to the AI Calling Bot Service to dial the buyer using personalized talking points.

Example AI Bot calls Amit: introduces Tower B corner flats, confirms his budget, and invites him for a site visit.
5 State Change & Promotion

If buyer expresses interest, call logs "State Change = Yes", updates CRM status to Qualified, and unlocks Stage 02.

Example Amit confirms: "Yes, I will visit this Saturday at 11 AM." Lead auto-promoted to Stage 02 (Site Visit).
Active Connectors: Portal/Meta Ingestion, Database Entity Matcher, AI Calling Bot Gateway, WhatsApp Cloud
✓ Milestone: Qualified Lead with Confirmed Site Visit Intent
Stage 02 Site Visit & Property Tour Flow
In Progress
1 Preferred Slot Booking

System reserves buyer's preferred date & time slot in the experience centre calendar to prevent double-booking.

Example Slot reserved: Saturday, 25th Oct at 11:00 AM at Vida Experience Center for Amit Sharma & family.
2 Instant WhatsApp Invite

WhatsApp Connector delivers confirmation with Google Maps location pin, digital QR gate pass, and project brochure.

Example WhatsApp delivered: "Welcome Amit! Here is your Visit Pass #VH-842, GPS Map link, and Tower B E-Brochure."
3 RM Assignment

System assigns a dedicated on-ground Relationship Manager (RM) and forwards the buyer's full profile dossier.

Example Assigned: RM Vikram Singh receives briefing note: "Amit prefers higher floors (12+), park view, ₹1.5 Cr budget."
4 Physical Sample Flat Tour

Customer visits site; RM guides them through actual sample apartment, tower lobbies, construction quality, and amenities.

Example Tour completed: Amit walks through Unit 1402 (Tower B), inspects master bedroom views and modern layout.
5 Visit Feedback & Shortlist

RM logs buyer feedback, shortlisted floor plans, and pricing discussions into database, transitioning toward booking.

Example Feedback logged: "Customer shortlisted Flat B-1402, requested customized payment plan" → Transition to Stage 03.
Active Connectors: WhatsApp API, Tour Database (domain/tour), Executive Roster Engine
✓ Milestone: Completed Site Visit & Unit Shortlisted
Stage 03 Booking & Token Advance Flow
In Progress
1 Unit Selection & Lock

Buyer chooses specific flat; backend instantly places a real-time lock on that unit in inventory to prevent duplicate booking.

Example Unit B-1402 (Tower B, 14th Floor, 1,850 sq.ft) marked "Temporarily Reserved - Holding for 48 Hours".
2 Official Cost Sheet

Pricing engine calculates base rate, floor rise, parking allotment, clubhouse fee, and generates a milestone cost sheet.

Example Cost Sheet: Total agreement value ₹1.48 Cr + GST, including 2 covered car parks and installment schedule.
3 Token Advance Payment

System generates secure payment link or logs bank NEFT/RTGS transaction to collect the initial booking advance.

Example Payment captured: ₹2,00,000 token advance paid online via NetBanking; instant bank reference generated.
4 Digital Receipt & Allotment

Document engine generates official digital booking receipt and provisional allotment letter, dispatched via email & WhatsApp.

Example Booking docket #VIDA-BK-912 dispatched with digital signature, receipt, and builder allotment stamp.
5 Digital KYC Verification

Buyer securely uploads PAN card, Aadhaar, and photos via portal; system verifies credentials and seals booking status.

Example KYC verified: PAN & Aadhaar authenticated. Unit B-1402 officially marked "Sold / In-Progress" → Sent to Accounts.
Active Connectors: Unit Inventory Database, Payment Gateway, CRM Deal Pipeline, KYC Portal
✓ Milestone: Unit Officially Booked & Token Advance Received
Stage 04 Procurement & Material Delivery Flow
In Progress
1 Construction Milestone Demand

Civil engineering team logs structural stage completion (e.g. 14th slab cast), triggering material requisitions for unit finishing.

Example Milestone trigger: Tower B 14th slab cast; requisition raised for Unit B-1402 internal wiring & tiling.
2 Vendor Purchase Orders (PO)

Procurement module issues purchase orders to verified suppliers for vitrified tiles, sanitary fixtures, conduits, and doors.

Example PO #PO-8819 dispatched: 450 sq.m Italian vitrified tiles from Kajaria and CP sanitary fittings from Kohler.
3 Site Delivery & Inspection

Materials arrive at project warehouse; site quality inspectors check specifications against standards and log batch clearance.

Example Inspection logged: Tile consignment Batch #T-402 inspected for zero cracks and uniform shade; approved.
4 Interior Fit-out Execution

Contractors execute internal plumbing, electrical cabling, floor tiling, wall plastering, and ceiling works inside the flat.

Example Unit B-1402 update: Flooring completed, bathroom fittings installed, first coat of primer applied.
5 Pre-Handover Quality Sign-off

Chief site engineer conducts 85-point inspection (pressure tests, electrical lines, door alignments) and issues unit clearance.

Example Certificate issued: Unit B-1402 certified 100% physically complete and ready for customer walkthrough.
Active Connectors: Procurement Repository (domain/procurement), Site Quality Ledger, Inventory ERP
✓ Milestone: Unit Construction & Fit-out 100% Certified
Stage 05 Accounts & Financial Billing Flow
Connector Pending
1 Milestone Demand Notice

As structural stages are certified by site engineers, billing engine automatically generates the next installment demand note.

Example Demand raised: "Tower B 14th Slab Completed — 10% installment of ₹14.80 Lakhs payable by 15th Nov".
2 Digital Invoice & Payment Link

System dispatches GST-compliant tax invoice along with a direct 1-click NetBanking/UPI payment link via WhatsApp and email.

Example WhatsApp sent to Amit: "Invoice #INV-1108 ready. Pay securely via Axis Bank Virtual Account Link."
3 Bank Credit & Instant Receipt

Once funds reach the escrow account, the payment connector auto-reconciles bank UTR numbers and dispatches stamped receipt.

Example Payment cleared: ₹14,80,000 credited; receipt #RCPT-7729 automatically sent to customer within 60 seconds.
4 Corporate Ledger Sync (Tally)

Payment credits, GST splits, and builder ledger vouchers are formatted for push into financial software (Pending Client Tally API).

Example Status: Accounting vouchers formatted; awaiting client IT team to provide Tally API access credentials.
5 Payment Nurture & Reminders

For unpaid installments, automated polite reminder schedules via WhatsApp and SMS keep buyers updated without harsh manual calls.

Example Automated note: Friendly reminder sent 3 days before due date with breakdown of remaining balance.
Active Connectors: Tally ERP Connector (Pending Client Credentials), Invoicing Engine, Payment Gateway Webhook
✓ Milestone: Installment Collected & Ledger Reconciled
Stage 06 Post-Sales & Possession Handover Flow
In Progress
1 Financial Clearance & NOC

Finance department verifies 100% cost sheet settlement, meter connection charges, and society sinking fund, issuing a Final NOC.

Example NOC Cleared: All installments, maintenance corpus, and legal documentation fees settled at ₹0 balance.
2 Joint Snag Walkthrough

Buyer and customer-care engineer inspect the finished flat room-by-room; any minor cosmetic touch-up is logged in mobile app.

Example Walkthrough: Amit inspects Unit B-1402; notes minor silicone touch-up on shower glass and railing polish.
3 Snag Rectification & Approval

Dedicated on-site snag rectification team completes all logged items within 48-72 hours; buyer reviews and signs digital acceptance.

Example Rectification done: All 2 punch-list items resolved; Amit inspects and signs digital clearance on tablet.
4 Key Handover & Possession Kit

Official handover ceremony: homeowner receives original unit brass keys, appliance warranty cards, blueprints, and Possession Letter.

Example Handover: Possession Certificate #POSS-2025-412 signed; brass keys and welcome gift hamper presented.
5 Community Onboarding

New homeowner is enrolled into the Vida Resident Mobile App for digital visitor entry gate-passes, clubhouse booking, and society dues.

Example Onboarded: Amit's profile activated on Vida Community App with Flat B-1402 profile and EV parking slot #P2-18.
Active Connectors: Handover Certificate Generator, Resident Portal, WhatsApp Community Bot
✓ Milestone: Keys Handed Over & Resident Welcomed
